❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is DiagnostiX a replacement for medical diagnosis? ▼
No, DiagnostiX is an AI-powered preliminary screening tool. It should not be considered a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult with a qualified healthcare professional for accurate diagnosis and treatment.
How accurate is the AI model? ▼
Our machine learning model achieved approximately 90% accuracy on validated medical datasets. However, accuracy can vary based on input data quality and individual variations. We continuously work to improve our models.
Is my health data secure? ▼
Yes! We use JWT authentication, encrypted password storage, and secure data handling practices. Your health data is protected and never shared with third parties without your consent.
Can I access DiagnostiX on mobile? ▼
Yes! DiagnostiX is fully responsive and works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. A dedicated mobile app is coming soon!
What languages are supported? ▼
Currently, we support English and हिंदी (Hindi). We're continuously adding more languages. You can easily switch languages using the selector in the top right corner.
How do I view my assessment history? ▼
After logging in, go to your Dashboard and click on "History". You'll see all your previous assessments with dates, risk levels, and risk percentages. This helps you track your health over time.
